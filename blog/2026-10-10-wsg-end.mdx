# The Last Days of WSG

Hey @everyone,
I wanted to let all of you know that as of today, the Wasteland Survival Guide is done. We are stepping away from it, and from now on, it will be provided as is. No future updates are planned for it, aside from extremely critical fixes. The WSG channels from Discord will be archived as well.
{/* truncate */}
It's been a good run, met a lot of cool people thanks to this endeavour, been through many good times, some bad as well, but overall it's all been worth it.
I want to say thank you to everyone who contributed, made suggestions, helped fix mistakes and just generally cheered me and the rest of the WSG team on, as we worked to deliver what was, for a long time, the "definitive" TTW modding guide.
If you still feel like using it, fret not, as it will remain up indefinitely. It may go down sometime in the future for one reason or another, but we'll cross that bridge when we get there.

Thank you all for everything,
**Audixas**
